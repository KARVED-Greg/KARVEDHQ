KARVED ("we", "us", "our") is a men's performance coaching business operated by Greg Lingard, based in Australia. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you interact with karved.com.au (the "Site"), our programs, or our marketing.

1. Who we are

KARVED, operated by Greg Lingard, Australia. Contact: greg@karved.com.au.

2. Information we collect

2.1 Information you provide

  • Name, email address, phone number

  • Communications with us (email, WhatsApp, SMS, phone calls)

2.2 Information collected automatically

  • IP address, device type, browser, operating system

  • Deliver our coaching services and digital resources
  • Personalise your experience (e.g., pillar-specific content based on quiz results)
  • Communicate with you (transactional messages, reminders, and marketing where you've opted in)
  • Process payments and manage subscriptions
  • Improve our services and content
  • Deliver targeted advertising on Meta, Google, and other platforms
  • Comply with legal obligations

4. Legal basis for processing

For visitors in jurisdictions with data protection laws (EU/UK), we rely on:

  • Consent — for marketing communications
  • Contract — to deliver services you've purchased
  • Legitimate interests — to improve our services and prevent misuse
  • Legal obligation — where required by law

5. Who we share your information with

We work with the following third-party providers, who process your data on our behalf:

  • HighLevel — customer relationship management, email and SMS delivery
  • Calendly — appointment scheduling
  • Zoom — coaching call delivery
  • Vidalytics — video hosting and analytics
  • Stripe or equivalent — payment processing
  • Google (Analytics, Ads) — website analytics and advertising

We do not sell your personal information. We may disclose information if required by law, subpoena, or to protect our rights and safety.

We use cookies and similar technologies to keep the Site functional, understand how visitors use the Site, and deliver targeted advertising. You can control cookies through your browser settings. Blocking cookies may affect site functionality.

7. Data retention

We retain your information for as long as necessary to provide services, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements. When no longer required, information is securely deleted or anonymised.

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • Opt out of marketing communications at any time
  • Withdraw consent (where consent is the legal basis)

To exercise any of these rights, email greg@karved.com.au.

We implement reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information. No system is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.
