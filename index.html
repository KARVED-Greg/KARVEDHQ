The KARVED method

No two strategies are the same.

There is no one-size-fits-all physique. Every body, every schedule, every life is different. So we don't bolt you to a template. We figure out which strategy works for the man in front of us, then we build the system around that.

The Bachelor's Approach

For the single guy working long hours. Fewer external commitments means more control over your training and meal prep, so we structure aggressively around your work and lean into hard pushes when the runway is clear.

The Family Man

Kids and family dinners are non-negotiable. We front-load your week and your day, so you've hit the work before evening — then sit down with the family without stressing about macros.

The Guy on the Move

FIFO, travel, unpredictable rosters. We build strategies that move with your calendar, not against it. You stay on track when your environment changes daily.

The Operator

Business owners, founders, executives. Energy and recovery are inputs to your work, not separate. We coach physique alongside sleep, stress, and cognitive load — because at this level they're inseparable.

Whichever shape your life takes, the underlying engineering is the same: preparation, accountability, and weekly adjustment. The strategy bends. The standards don't.

Meet Greg

I built this because I lived it.

I was always the small kid. Couldn't put on size no matter what I tried, and I convinced myself I had "bad genetics." The reality was much simpler — my approach wasn't right.

Competitive rugby gave me the foundation: size, power, work ethic. But it didn't give me the aesthetic physique I was chasing. I looked like a rugby player, not like someone who trained for how they looked.

Six years ago I pivoted. Shifted my focus entirely to physique optimisation and performance. The results went insane. Not because I suddenly got "better genetics" — because I finally had the right system.

That same system became KARVED. Twelve years of trial, error, and refinement across my own body and 100+ clients who started in exactly the position you might be in right now.

The point of this business isn't to sell you a program. It's to be the coach I wish I'd had when I was wasting years on bad strategy. Everything we do flows from that.

Greg Lingard

Founder, KARVED
